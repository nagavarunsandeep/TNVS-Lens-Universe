Water Quality Parameters Explained

pH

Measures how acidic or alkaline water is. A range of 6.5 to 8.5 is generally acceptable for most fish and for drinking water.

Salinity

The concentration of dissolved salts in water, measured in parts per thousand (ppt). Crucial for distinguishing between freshwater, brackish, and marine environments.

Total Dissolved Solids (TDS)

Represents the total concentration of dissolved substances in water. For drinking water, a TDS level under 500 ppm is recommended.

Hardness

Primarily the amount of dissolved calcium and magnesium. Water is classified from soft to very hard. Moderately hard water is often preferred for drinking.
