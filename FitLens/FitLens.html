Food Recommendations

Here are some food suggestions based on macronutrient groups. Adjust portion sizes to meet your daily calorie goals.

Foods Rich in Protein

  • Lean Meats: Chicken Breast, Turkey, Lean Beef
  • Fish: Salmon, Tuna, Cod
  • Dairy: Greek Yogurt, Cottage Cheese, Milk
  • Eggs: A versatile and complete protein source.
  • Plant-Based: Lentils, Chickpeas, Tofu, Edamame, Quinoa

Foods Rich in Healthy Carbohydrates

  • Whole Grains: Oats, Brown Rice, Quinoa, Whole-Wheat Bread
  • Vegetables: Sweet Potatoes, Broccoli, Spinach, Bell Peppers
  • Fruits: Berries, Bananas, Apples, Oranges
  • Legumes: Beans, Lentils, Peas

Foods Rich in Healthy Fats

  • Avocados: Rich in monounsaturated fats.
  • Nuts & Seeds: Almonds, Walnuts, Chia Seeds, Flaxseeds
  • Oils: Extra Virgin Olive Oil, Coconut Oil
  • Fatty Fish: Salmon, Mackerel, Sardines (also high in protein)

Special Diet for Gym Persons

For individuals who are regularly training at the gym, protein intake is crucial for muscle repair and growth. Consider a higher protein percentage in your diet (e.g., 30-40% of total calories).

  • Pre-Workout (30-60 mins before): A combination of simple carbs and a small amount of protein. Example: A banana with a tablespoon of peanut butter, or a small bowl of oatmeal.
  • Post-Workout (within 60 mins after): A meal rich in protein and complex carbs to replenish glycogen stores and repair muscle. Example: A protein shake, grilled chicken with sweet potato, or Greek yogurt with berries.
  • Hydration: Drink plenty of water throughout the day, especially before, during, and after your workout.
